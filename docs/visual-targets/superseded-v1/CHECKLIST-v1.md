# Visual target checklist

Compare real browser captures against these features, side by side with the PNGs in this folder. These are design targets, not screenshots. "Must" items define the look. "Nice" items are optional.

## Shared across all three targets

- **Camera**
  - 3/4 isometric orbit, about 35–45° down, looking at the slab's front-left corner.
  - The slab fills about 80% of the frame width.
  - The top surface and the front cutaway face are both clearly visible.
- **Silhouette**
  - One floating rectangular slab with crisp vertical side faces, hanging in a dark starry void.
  - A few dark rock chunks drift around it (nice).
  - Nothing extends past the slab except smoke and steam.
- **Cutaway (must)**
  - The front face glows from within: an orange-red mantle with yellow-orange convection ribbons and flow arrows.
  - A bright white-yellow core glow sits at the bottom.
  - Crust strata are tan, brown and cream bands.
  - The cutaway is the brightest thing in the frame.
- **Materials**
  - Green grass and dark tree clumps.
  - Turquoise-to-deep-blue water with white surf at the edges.
  - Cream limestone, dark basalt, and tan/brown crust blocks with visible block seams.
  - Overall stylized, rich and slightly chunky, not photoreal and not flat.
- **Lighting**
  - Warm golden sun from back-right with long soft shadows.
  - Magma rim-light on the cutaway.
  - The sky behind the slab is a sunset gradient blending into the starry void.
- **Characters**
  - Pebble-people: round smooth grey/sand stones with big white eyes, an open happy mouth, stubby arms and legs, and green leaf scarves.
  - They are about 1/15 of the slab depth tall and readable at a glance.
  - They react in groups: cheer with arms up, or panic and duck.
- **Houses**
  - Small cream or grey stone cottages with tile or thatch roofs and warm-lit windows.
  - The "413" variant carries a green shield badge with white "413".
- **HUD (Hebrew RTL, navy #1b2440-ish and cream #f3ead8-ish, chunky rounded, soft drop shadows)**
  - Top-left: two round navy buttons with white rings, pause and mute.
  - Top-right: a navy pill with the level name in white bold Hebrew and 3 gold stars to its left.
  - Bottom-center/left: a cream panel with the "מיליוני שנים" slider. It has a navy track, a round blue knob, and tick labels on a non-linear scale.
  - A navy readout box sits next to the slider: `rate × time = distance` in LTR math inside RTL text.
  - Bottom-right: a cream tray with 3 square chunky buttons, each with an icon and a label underneath, in RTL order from the right: החלקה, משיכה, דחיפה.
    - Icons: `→|←` for push, `←→` for pull, `⇄` for slide.
    - The selected button has a navy fill, a white icon and a glowing cyan ring.
- **Feedback language**
  - Glowing orange seams on active cracks.
  - Dust puffs on cliff edges and collapses.
  - Steam wisps where magma meets water.
  - Smoke rings from volcanoes.
  - Comic shake lines around shaking things.
