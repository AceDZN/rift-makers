## Host notes

- `?level=N` (1–18) opens the level directly, even when it is locked in the local save. Each level still opens on its short intro card (one tap or Enter), which states the goal; nothing else stands between the link and play.
- Level 3 has three prediction rounds inside the one level; `level-complete` comes after the third.
- Level 18 is the final: no hints by design.
- Planet commissions (generated puzzles, ids 101–109, unlocked after the final) post nothing to the host.
